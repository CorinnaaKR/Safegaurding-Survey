H.E.L.I. — Independent Research

Safeguarding Training:
Your Experience & What Could Be Better

Safeguarding training is an important part of working with children and young people, but everyone's experience of it is different.

I'm carrying out some independent research to better understand how people experience safeguarding training, what works well, and where there may be opportunities to improve it.

This short survey should take approximately 5–7 minutes to complete. You do not need to share details of any specific safeguarding cases or identify individuals.

Section 6 of 7

Introducing the Concept

Thank you for sharing your experience.

Based on some of the challenges identified through this research, I am exploring the development of an interactive educational platform called H.E.L.I. (Helping Everyone Learn Interactively). H.E.L.I. is intended to use interactive scenarios, decision-making and storytelling to help learners explore potentially difficult situations in a safe environment.

The concept is still in development, and this survey is not asking you to endorse the project. I'm interested in whether the approach addresses some of the needs you have identified.
